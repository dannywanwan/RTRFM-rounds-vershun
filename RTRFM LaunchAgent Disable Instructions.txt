RTRFM LaunchAgent disable / uninstall instructions

To disable the weekly scheduled run:

launchctl bootout gui/$(id -u) ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.daniel.rtrfm.plist

To confirm it is no longer loaded:

launchctl print gui/$(id -u)/com.daniel.rtrfm

If it is unloaded, launchctl should report that it cannot find the service.

To fully remove the LaunchAgent plist after disabling it:

rm ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.daniel.rtrfm.plist

This does not delete the project, the script, playlists, or log files.
